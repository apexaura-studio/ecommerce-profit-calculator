Complete E-Commerce Profit, Return & Payment Optimization Guide

To run a successful D2C brand or marketplace store in India, calculating standard product margins is only the first step. Sellers must actively manage RTO freight drain, inventory write-offs, and optimize Prepaid conversion incentives.

Why Giving Prepaid Discounts Makes You More Money

COD orders carry high courier handling surcharges and an average 25%–35% failure rate. By offering an instant ₹30–₹50 discount for UPI/Card payments, you avoid reverse shipping fees, courier COD fees, and locked capital—resulting in significantly higher net profits.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between RTO and Customer Return?

RTO (Return to Origin) occurs when an undelivered parcel comes back from the customer doorstep before delivery. Customer Return happens after the customer accepts, opens, and later requests a return.

How do I calculate the maximum safe discount for prepaid orders?

Calculate your COD surcharge plus expected RTO shipping loss per unit. Any discount lower than this combined total directly adds to your bottom-line profit.

How to Calculate Real E-Commerce Profit Margin?

Most new online sellers fail because they ignore the heavy cost of RTO (Return to Origin). When an order is returned or rejected at the customer's doorstep, the seller still pays for forward shipping, reverse shipping charges, and damaged packaging materials while generating zero revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is RTO in e-commerce?

RTO stands for Return to Origin. It happens when a Cash on Delivery (COD) or prepaid parcel fails to be delivered and returns back to the seller warehouse.

How can I reduce RTO loss on Meesho & Amazon?

Verify customer contact details via WhatsApp automated confirmation, encourage prepaid orders by offering small discounts, and avoid risky pincodes with poor courier delivery records.
